### H2: How does JPG compression work? (brief technical explainer, 200 words)

- Lossy compression: throws away data your eyes won't notice
- Chroma subsampling (4:2:0 vs 4:4:4)
- Quality factor: 60-80 is the sweet spot
- "Target size" mode: binary search on quality
- Link to deeper explainer: [How JPEG compression works](/blog/how-jpeg-compression-works) (Day 43)

### H2: JPG vs JPEG vs JFIF — what's the difference?

- Same format, different extensions
- JPG = Windows shorthand, JPEG = spec name
- All three behave identically
- Tool handles all three
